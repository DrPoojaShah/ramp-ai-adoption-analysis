Business AI adoption jumped 12 points in 2026, and Anthropic grew with it

The share of US businesses paying for AI rose from 44.4% in December 2025 to 56.8% in October 2026, after stalling in late 2025. Over the same months, Anthropic's share went from 17.8% to 44.8% and passed OpenAI in May 2026, while OpenAI's held near 40%.

By Pooja Shah · Data: Ramp AI Index, 50,000+ US businesses, pulled November 2026
Share of US businesses on Ramp paying for each AI vendor, by month. Vendor shares overlap because one business can pay several vendors. Hover the chart for monthly values.

Does it hold up?

Yes. The 2026 jump appears for small, medium and large businesses alike, after all three were flat from August to December 2025.

Company sizeDec 2025Oct 2026Change

Size still matters: large businesses lead small ones by about 15 points, and that gap did not close in 2026.

Why did it happen?

Two explanations fit the headline. The data supports both, but the second accounts for more of the growth.

1. New adopters

Businesses that paid for no AI started paying in 2026.

Can explain at most the 12.4-point rise in overall adoption.

2. Existing users added a vendor

Businesses already paying for AI added Anthropic as a second tool.

Anthropic gained 27.0 points, so at least 14.6 points came from existing AI buyers.
The overlap check. For any two groups, the share paying both is at least OpenAI + Anthropic − overall. At least 28.6% of businesses paid both vendors in October 2026, up from at least 14.3% in December 2025. OpenAI's share didn't fall, so businesses were adding, not switching.

Still unresolved: which vendor the new adopters chose, and why buying changed in 2026. Product launches, pricing or Ramp's own customer mix could all play a part, and this data cannot tell them apart.

So what

AI buying is becoming multi-vendor. Finance and IT teams should budget for more than one AI subscription per team and review overlapping seats. For AI vendors, most 2026 growth came from winning businesses that already use a rival. Small businesses still trail large ones by about 15 points, so they are the largest group not yet buying.

Data notes

  • Source: Ramp AI Index via the free Ramp Data connector. Each figure is the share of US businesses on Ramp with paid spend on an AI tool that month.
  • Only spending through Ramp cards and bill pay is counted; free tiers and tools paid another way are missed.
  • Ramp reports no Google data after June 2023, so Google is left out.
